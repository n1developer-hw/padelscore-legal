Features

Designed for round screens and outdoor visibility.

One‑tap scoring

Tap Team 1 or Team 2 to award a point. The current points, games, sets, and serving indicator stay clear.

Auto‑save & resume

Match state is periodically saved so you can resume after a restart and keep playing.

Padel/Tennis rules

Best of 3/5 sets, tie‑break at 6–6, and configurable deuce mode (Advantage, Golden Point, or Win by 2).

Local match history

Stores recent completed matches locally on the watch (date, duration, final sets score). No cloud sync.

Undo / pause / finish

Undo the last point, pause the match timer, or finish the match with a confirmation prompt.

Offline by design

No accounts, no analytics, and no network communication required for normal operation.

How it works

Get started in 5 simple steps and focus on the match

01

Install PadelScore

WearOS is currently in closed beta; see the Download section to join and install it. iOS is coming soon.

02

Open & play

Tap Start. If there's an unfinished match, you can resume it.

03

Match setup

Select best‑of format, optional time limit, and deuce mode (Advantage / Golden Point / Win by 2).

FAQ

Quick answers.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. PadelScore is designed for offline use. Match state and history are stored locally on the watch.

Will I lose my match if the app closes?

No. The match is periodically auto‑saved, and you can resume after restart.

Which scoring rules are supported?

0/15/30/40 scoring, tie‑break at 6–6, and configurable deuce mode: Advantage, Golden Point, or Win by 2.

Do you collect data?

No. The app does not send data to the developer, does not use analytics, and does not require an account. See Privacy Policy.
