PadelScore Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29
← Home
This policy covers the PadelScore WearOS closed beta, its Google Groups tester sign-up, and this website. Match data stays on the watch. Limited data is processed by Google for beta access and by GitHub Pages for website security, as described below.

1. Who is responsible

PadelScore is a wearable application currently in closed beta for WearOS, with iOS coming soon. The data controller is Mykola Tkach, an individual developer based in Spain. Contact details are in section 11.

2. App data and offline operation

PadelScore is designed to work offline. The app does not require an internet connection for normal scoring. Match information is stored locally on the watch and is not sent to the developer.

To provide core functionality, PadelScore stores the following data locally on the watch:

  • Match scoring data (points/games/sets, selected rules, timer) to allow auto-save and resume
  • Match history (e.g., last ~10 matches) including date, duration, and final sets score

The app does not use analytics, ads, tracking technologies, or third-party SDKs. The developer does not receive match scores or match history.

3. WearOS beta participation

To get access to the closed beta, you join the linked Google Group and opt in through Google Play. Google processes your Google Account information and participation under its own privacy terms. The group owner (the developer) can see member information made available in Google Groups, which may include your email address, profile name, and membership status. Other group members may see profile or membership information depending on the group’s privacy settings.

The developer uses group membership information only to verify beta eligibility and administer the test. The legal basis is the developer’s legitimate interest in restricting access to and operating the closed beta (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Participation is voluntary. You can leave the group and contact the developer to exercise your rights. Beta feedback you choose to send may also include personal information; please do not include sensitive information. Feedback is kept only as long as needed to handle it and improve or support the beta, then deleted or anonymised where possible.

The developer will remove your tester membership and delete any exported tester-list copies under their control within 30 days after the beta ends or you leave the group. Google may retain information in its services under its own retention practices. Google states that information may be processed on servers outside the country where you live and describes the transfer safeguards it uses in its Privacy Policy and data transfer frameworks. See also the Google Groups privacy settings.

4. Website, hosting, and cookies

This is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. The website code does not set or read cookies, use browser storage, run analytics, or embed third-party content. GitHub states that GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub handles this data under its own Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data collection notice.

Links to Google Groups and Google Play open external services. When you follow a link, those services may process technical and account information under their own privacy terms. The PadelScore website does not receive your Google Account details just because you visit it or click a link.

5. Permissions

PadelScore requests only the minimum system permissions required for proper operation on a wearable device, such as:

  • Display-related permissions
  • Local device storage (to save match state and history on the watch)

PadelScore does not request permissions for contacts, location, microphone, or camera.

6. Data recipients

Match data remains on your watch. For beta access and website hosting, Google and GitHub process the limited information described above under their respective privacy terms. The developer does not sell personal data or use beta contact details for marketing.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete match data by clearing match history in the app or removing the app / clearing its data in system settings. To stop beta participation, leave the Google Group. You can also contact the developer to request access to or deletion of beta contact details held by the developer.

8. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and request data portability where applicable. Contact the developer using the details below. You may also lodge a complaint with your competent data protection supervisory authority. In Spain, this is the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

9. Children’s privacy

PadelScore is not directed to children. If you believe a child has joined the beta group, contact the developer so the membership can be reviewed and removed.

10. Changes to this policy

If changes are made to this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be made available at the same URL.
