PadelScore Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-24
← Home
This policy applies to the current version of PadelScore. It describes offline operation and local storage on the watch. No data is sent to the developer.

1. Introduction

PadelScore is a wearable application designed for Huawei Watch devices. The application allows users to track scores for padel and tennis matches directly on their smartwatch.

2. Offline operation

PadelScore is designed to work fully offline. The app does not require an internet connection for normal use.

3. Data processed and stored on the device

PadelScore does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer or third parties.

To provide core functionality, PadelScore stores some data locally on the watch, such as:

  • Match scoring data (points/games/sets, selected rules, timer) to allow auto-save and resume
  • Match history (e.g., last ~10 matches) including date, duration, and final sets score

The app does not use analytics, ads, tracking technologies, or third-party SDKs.

4. Permissions

PadelScore requests only the minimum system permissions required for proper operation on a wearable device, such as:

  • Display-related permissions
  • Local device storage (to save match state and history on the watch)

PadelScore does not request permissions for contacts, location, microphone, or camera.

5. Data sharing

PadelScore does not share any data with third parties.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete stored data by clearing match history inside the app (if available) or by removing the app / clearing its data via system settings.

7. Children’s privacy

PadelScore does not target children specifically and does not collect personal data from users of any age.

8. Changes to this policy

If changes are made to this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be made available at the same URL.

9. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact:

  • Email: padelscore.top@gmail.com

GDPR — Data subject rights

Since PadelScore does not transmit personal data to the developer and processing is local to the device, you can exercise your rights by managing your data directly on your watch (viewing, clearing history, or uninstalling the app).
